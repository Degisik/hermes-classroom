Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

Hermes Classroom requests read-only access to Google Classroom data (courses, the signed-in user's coursework and announcements) for the account that authorizes it.

  • Data is used only to show the authorizing user their own coursework, deadlines and announcements.
  • Data is processed on the owner's private server and is not sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties.
  • Data is not used for advertising or to train generalized AI/ML models.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's server. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Hermes Classroom's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Degisik/hermes-classroom/issues.
